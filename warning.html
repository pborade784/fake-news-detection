⚠️ Warning Signs of Fake News

Look for these signs before trusting online information.

🚨 Clickbait Headlines

Headlines that use shocking or exaggerated words to attract attention.

❓ Unknown Source

Be careful when information comes from an unknown or unreliable website.

😡 Emotional Language

Excessive fear, anger or excitement can be used to influence readers.

🖼️ Misleading Images

Images may be edited, manipulated or taken from a different event.

⏰ Urgent Messages

Messages asking you to immediately forward or share information should be checked carefully.

📄 No Evidence

Claims without reliable evidence, sources or supporting information should be verified.
