📚 Media Literacy

Learn how to evaluate information before believing or sharing it.

1️⃣ Check the Source

Find out who published the information. Prefer reliable and well-known sources.

2️⃣ Check the Date

Check when the article or post was published. Old information may be presented as new.

3️⃣ Read the Full Article

Do not believe information only from a headline. Read the complete article.

4️⃣ Verify Images

Images can be edited or taken from another event. Check whether the image is genuine.

5️⃣ Compare Sources

Compare important information with multiple trustworthy sources.

6️⃣ Think Before Sharing

Take a moment to verify information before forwarding it to others.
