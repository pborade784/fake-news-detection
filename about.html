ℹ️ About the Project

Fake News Detection & Media Literacy Programme

Purpose of the Project

The purpose of this project is to create awareness about fake news and encourage responsible use of digital media.

Objectives

• To create awareness about fake news.
• To teach basic news verification techniques.
• To promote media literacy.
• To encourage responsible sharing of information.

Importance

False information can spread quickly through social media and messaging platforms. Media literacy helps people think critically and verify information before sharing it.

CEP Project

This website has been developed as part of a Community Engagement Project to promote awareness and responsible digital behaviour.
